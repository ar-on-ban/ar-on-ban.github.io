gb-gws

gb-gws is a personal Google OAuth client application used exclusively by Aron Banyasz.

Notice: This is not a public service or product. This OAuth app is used only by Aron's own assistants and the gws command-line tool to access his personal Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Google Docs.

Purpose

The gb-gws application enables authorized access to Aron's personal Google Workspace data for automation and productivity tools that he personally controls and operates.
